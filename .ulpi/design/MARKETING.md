No automatic outreach, posting, spending or third-party mutations. Preserve all existing CMS routes and content. Sidebar grouped into Content, Marketing, Operations and Administration. Reduce border noise, use restrained backgrounds and semantic selection. Responsive lists and native forms. Unique weekly task generation is an explicit owner action. Marketing XP is capped and reversible; do not equate self-reports with independently verified commercial performance.
