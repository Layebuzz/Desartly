# Airbnb · Unlock Adventure presentation

The supplied project photographs were used as visual references, not placed directly on the site. Five new interior/destination scenes and additional Kuwait City placement settings were generated for this portfolio presentation. `brand-reference.png` is the supplied logo reference; `scripts/build-airbnb-assets.mjs` extracts the white client lockup and aligns the campaign headline and logo directly inside the published visuals. Published WebP files are opaque.

The two outdoor scenes are **presentation mockups**, not documentary photographs of actual placements. The project credit on the page identifies them accordingly.

## Image directions

- `coastal-bedroom.png`: A familiar bedroom opens onto a turquoise coastal cove and flamingo. Used as the wide campaign introduction.
- `arctic-bedroom.png`: Polar bear and snow landscape enter a warm bedroom.
- `rainforest-bedroom.png`: Rhinoceros at the threshold of a rainforest bedroom.
- `savannah-bedroom.png`: Giraffe and sunrise savannah beyond a quiet interior.
- `antarctic-bedroom.png`: Penguin and Antarctic seascape beyond a city bedroom.
- `kuwait-city-display.png`: Blank, front-facing plaza display for a precise brand-art application.
- `kuwait-bus-shelter.png`: Blank, front-facing shelter display for a second application.
- `kuwait-blue-hour-streetboard.png`: Printed polar-bear billboard with visible vinyl tension and creases at blue hour. The complete lockup is already aligned in this generated scene.
- `kuwait-rhino-streetboard-base.png`: Printed rhinoceros streetboard; the mark was cleared for precise baseline placement in the asset builder.
- `kuwait-giraffe-lightbox-base.png`: Reflective bus-shelter print with uneven fluorescent transmission; the mark was cleared for baseline placement.
- `kuwait-penguin-lightbox-base.png`: Night bus shelter with a visible tube in the frame and subtle glass reflections; the mark was cleared for baseline placement.

## Presentation benchmark

- [Pentagram / UAnimals](https://www.pentagram.com/work/uanimals): A simple idea gains range through a deliberately edited image family. Here the campaign's four destinations are presented as one controlled visual family.
- [Pentagram / OffLimits](https://www.pentagram.com/work/offlimits): A character-driven identity remains coherent when each execution changes subject and context. Here the consistent headline and Airbnb mark hold different worlds together.
- [COLLINS / Target A New Day](https://wearecollins.com/case-studies/target-a-new-day/): Warm imagery is supported by a clear emotional premise. Here one short editorial opening frames the visual gallery.
- [Studio Dumbar / MTV VMAs 2022](https://studiodumbar.com/work/mtv-vmas-2022): Campaign design should retain its identity across formats. Here the branded art is tested within upright outdoor and digital placements.

This case uses a different sequence from MCI and Divar: a square campaign cover → four-frame portrait gallery → coral narrative pause → one plaza display → a pair of printed streetboards → two backlit transit executions. No sketch imagery or captions under the pictures.
