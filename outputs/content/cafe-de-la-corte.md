# Café De La Corte

Packaging with a sense of craft.

**Discipline:** Branding / Packaging design  
**Role:** Packaging design and visual execution  
**Commission:** Through a Kuwait-based creative studio  
**Scope:** Material applications, label layouts, bottle treatments and product mockups  
**Credit:** The brand identity was established before my involvement.

## From identity to physical experience

Café De La Corte is a premium coffee brand built around craftsmanship, richness and refined taste. Commissioned through a Kuwait-based creative studio, I translated its existing identity into a cohesive packaging system.

My focus was the physical expression of the brand: how its mark sits on glass, how the bottle feels in hand, and how materials work together. The scope covered packaging design and visual execution, including label layouts, material applications, bottle treatments and product mockups.

## Refined, without losing its texture

The direction pairs clear glass and warm coffee tones with the rough weave of burlap and a simple twine fastening. A restrained label structure keeps the existing identity legible while allowing the materials and the product itself to carry much of the visual character.

The contrast is deliberate: clean graphic placement against an irregular, tactile surface. It gives the packaging a crafted character without relying on additional decoration.

## A consistent system across surfaces

The bottle, fabric wrap and label were treated as parts of one system. Scale, placement and material applications were developed with visual consistency and production feasibility in mind.

The existing brand mark remains the anchor. My contribution was to adapt it to these physical touchpoints while maintaining a clear hierarchy and a coherent premium presentation.

## From product detail to serving ritual

Close-up imagery reveals the label, glass and woven texture. Lifestyle mockups place the packaging within a serving occasion, showing how the same design language carries from an isolated product view into a richer setting.

The final presentation brings those views together: the overall silhouette, the material details and the product in context. These are design mockups and visual executions; no sales or production results are claimed.
